# 14. Search, Filter & Sort Flow

## 1. Compact Accordion System
- **Default State**: All accordion sections closed by default.
- **Single Open Section**: Opening an accordion automatically collapses previously open sections.
- **Filter Criteria**:
  - Categories & Subcategories
  - Brands (with real-time brand search & OR logic multi-checkboxes)
  - Price Range (numeric inputs & dual range preset pills)
  - Star Ratings (5★ to 1★+)
  - Discount & Availability
- **Independent Sorting**: Sort dropdown (Price, Ratings, Discount, Newest, Popular) operates independently from filter criteria.
- **Active Filter Chips**: Dismissible chips (`[ Electronics × ]`, `[ Apple × ]`) appearing only when filters are active.
