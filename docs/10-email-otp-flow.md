# 10. Email OTP Flow

- **Algorithm**: SecureRandom 6-digit number (`100000 - 999999`).
- **Expiry Duration**: 5 minutes (300 seconds) with real-time UI countdown timer.
- **Resend Cooldown**: 60 seconds rate limit to prevent spam.
- **UI Experience**: Auto-focus navigation, backspace movement, numeric validation, and paste support.
