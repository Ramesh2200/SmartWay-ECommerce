# 20. My Account & Orders Hub

- **Tabs**: Personal Information, My Orders, Saved Addresses, Account Security.
- **Order Cards**: Displays real commercial order numbers (`SW-YYYYMMDD-XXXXXX`), item images, totals, and live status badges.
- **Order Tracking**: Visual 6-step progress bar (`Placed` $ightarrow$ `Confirmed` $ightarrow$ `Processing` $ightarrow$ `Shipped` $ightarrow$ `Out for Delivery` $ightarrow$ `Delivered`).
- **Interactive Actions**: `[ Buy Again ]` and `[ Cancel Order ]` with stock restoration.
