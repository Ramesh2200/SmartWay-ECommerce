# 15. Product Details Flow

- Left: Large sharp centered product image, smooth hover zoom, thumbnail strip with active ring indicator.
- Right: `CATEGORY • BRAND` breadcrumbs, large title (32–44px), star ratings, in-stock count, quantity selector (`[ − ] 1 [ + ]`).
- CTAs: `[ 🛒 Add to Cart ]` and `[ ⚡ Buy Now ]`.
- Guest Flow: Clicking *Buy Now* unauthenticated prompts a login modal and redirects to checkout after authentication.
